Where the episodes live

HN Radio keeps its shows on one small disk. On September 4, 2026 that disk filled up and an episode did not go out. This is what was taking up the room, and what changed.

What we gave up: the spare PCM parts made it free to re-mix an old episode with new pacing or new music, and to swap one voice without re-recording the others. Those happen rarely enough that re-recording the whole episode on the day it is asked for costs less than storing every episode three times, forever.
