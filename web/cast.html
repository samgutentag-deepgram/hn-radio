The Cast

Every voice in the published Flux TTS catalog, on one grid. HN Radio is two people, twice a day: Alexis hosts the Morning Edition, Cole hosts the Afternoon Edition, and each of them has a co-host drawn from this page. Hover over a card, or press it, to hear that voice introduce itself. Each voice with an official Deepgram voice card wears it here, the same card you see beside that speaker in an episode script; the rest are shown by their initial.
